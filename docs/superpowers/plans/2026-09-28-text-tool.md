# The text tool

Text on the board, as Affinity lays it: **artistic text**, a line set at
a point that grows as it is typed and scales as a picture does, and
**frame text**, a box the words wrap inside and reflow in when it is
resized. One tool (`T`) makes both — the kind is chosen on the text's
own properties bar, and `T` again switches it, as Affinity's pair of
text tools share their key. A text is a layer of a new kind, `text`,
holding the one text it was made for, the way a vector layer holds its
object. Stacked on `app-menu` (#14). Test first, every time: a test
that fails, then the production code that makes it pass. Atomic,
semantic commits.

## What "done" is

Parity with what the design tools and the boards on the market do with
text, within one style per text:

- Click with the tool: artistic text at the point. Drag: artistic text
  whose size is the drag's height (Affinity), or a text frame of the
  dragged area. Nothing lands on the board until a character is typed,
  and a text left empty is taken off again.
- Editing: the caret, the selection, Shift with any movement, Ctrl a
  word at a time, Home/End the line and Ctrl+Home/End the text, the
  arrows up and down by line at the same x, Enter a new line, Ctrl+A,
  the clipboard (Ctrl+C/X/V as text), typing over a selection, a
  press placing the caret, a drag selecting, a double click a word and
  a triple click a paragraph; Esc or a press elsewhere ends it, leaving
  the text selected. Undo inside a text walks back the typing; the
  whole session is one step on the board's history.
- A double click on a text with the Select tool, or a click on one with
  the Text tool, opens it for editing where it was clicked.
- Resizing artistic text scales it (the size follows); resizing a text
  frame reflows it; both turn. A frame that cannot show all its text
  says so, as Affinity's red overflow mark does.
- Style: font family (the machine's, and a bundled Liberation Sans in
  four styles that every board can count on), size, bold, italic,
  underline, strikethrough, alignment (left, centre, right, justify),
  the frame's vertical alignment, line spacing, letter spacing, and the
  ink from the dock.
- Crisp at every zoom: glyphs rasterized at the size they are seen at,
  cached in a sheet of their own.
- The layers panel shows text layers as text, named after what they
  say until they are renamed; the filter knows the kind.
- The command line and the skill: `sinopia text` lists, adds and
  changes texts; a fragment may carry `text` elements; `board.md`
  lists what the texts say.

Deliberately not in this cut: styling a range of characters apart from
the rest of its text, per-character font fallback (a glyph a face does
not have is its replacement), shaping beyond kerning pairs, and text on
a path.

## Tasks

Model and pure core:

- [ ] `doc`: `Kind::Text`, `Element::Text` (artistic or frame, the
      string and its style), checked on the way in; a text stands on a
      text layer and a text layer holds only text.
- [ ] `fonts`: the bundled Liberation Sans in regular, bold, italic
      and bold italic; faces by family and style, loaded once, falling
      back to the bundled one; the machine's families from `fc-list`.
- [ ] `typeset`: lines (a newline breaks one; a frame wraps at its
      width), alignment and justification, leading, tracking, kerning;
      the caret's place, the place under a point, the bands a
      selection paints, line and word movement; the natural size of
      artistic text.
- [ ] `glyphs`: the world's glyph sheet — glyphs rasterized at the
      size they are seen at (a ladder of sizes), packed, the region
      that changed, and starting over when it is full.
- [ ] `scene`: a text is its glyphs, turned with it and cut by its
      frame; underline and strikethrough; a text frame shows only the
      lines that fit.
- [ ] `select`: a text's frame and hit; resizing artistic text scales
      its size, resizing frame text reflows it.
- [ ] `history`: the steps of one typing session are one step.
- [ ] `editor`: the Text tool — placing, the session, keys, the
      pointer, ending it, the layer named after the text.

Chrome:

- [ ] `props`: the brush's bar stands centred.
- [ ] `textbar`: the text's bar, centred like the brush's — kind,
      font, size, B I U S, alignment, vertical alignment, line and
      letter spacing.
- [ ] `dock`: the Text tool, its icon, `T`.
- [ ] `layers`: text rows (a T for a thumbnail), the filter's kind.

Shell:

- [ ] `gfx`: the glyph sheet as a texture written in place.
- [ ] `app`: typing and the pointer into a text, the caret and the
      selection drawn, the I-beam, the text bar and its font menu,
      the clipboard, the session as one step.

The door an agent asks through:

- [ ] `ipc`: `texts`, `add_text`, `set_text`; `layers` lists the kind.
- [ ] `cli`: `sinopia text list | add | set`.
- [ ] `export`: `board.md` lists the texts.
- [ ] `graft`: a fragment may carry texts.
- [ ] `skills/sinopia/SKILL.md`: text is drawable now.

Wrap-up:
